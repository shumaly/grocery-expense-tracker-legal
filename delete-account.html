Delete your account and data

This page is for users of Grocery Expense Tracker. You can request deletion even if you no longer have the app installed.

Delete data in the app

  1. Open Grocery Expense Tracker and sign in with the Google account whose data you want to delete.
  2. Open Account & privacy and tap Delete account data.
  3. Confirm the action. This removes that account's saved receipt photos, extracted items and in-app workbooks from this phone and signs you out. Other accounts' data on the same phone is not removed.

Deleting an individual receipt from the Receipts tab removes its linked items. Uninstalling the app removes its private local data from that phone. Excel files exported outside the app are separate files and must be deleted where you saved them.

Request deletion without the app

Email deepmwizard@gmail.com to request account and data deletion. Use the subject “Grocery Expense Tracker account deletion request.”

Please identify the Google account used with the app, but do not send passwords, identity documents, receipt photos or payment details. We will respond with any verification or next steps needed and review data held by our service that can be matched to your account. A deletion request does not require reinstalling the app.

Your receipt archive and purchase history are kept on your phone. Version 1.1 and later also use temporary cloud images, analysis results and job metadata to complete queued analysis. The in-app account deletion action cancels that account's temporary jobs and removes their cloud images and results; job metadata expires automatically. We cannot remotely erase files on a device we do not control; uninstalling the app removes its private local files from that device. Our service keeps a separate pseudonymous usage-limit record in Firestore; the in-app delete action does not automatically remove it or reset the daily allowance. We will review a verified request to remove server-held records we can identify and control.

Images previously sent to the external AI provider may remain under that provider's terms. Tell us if your request also concerns previously submitted receipt images; we can explain the available provider request path, but cannot promise remote deletion by the provider. See our privacy policy for details.

For help with any of these steps, email deepmwizard@gmail.com.
