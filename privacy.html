Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Grocery Expense Tracker Android app (com.sajjad.receiptreader).

Grocery Expense Tracker is operated by its developer, Sajjad Shumaly. Contact deepmwizard@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy explains the app's current behavior; we will update it if its data practices change.

What the app handles

  • Google sign-in: Google provides an identity token and basic account details such as name and email. The app sends the token to our HTTPS service to verify sign-in and obtain a temporary app session. Your name and email are displayed and stored in the app on your phone. The service uses your Google account identifier to authenticate requests and derive a pseudonymous account key; it does not maintain a profile database.
  • Receipt photos: You may take a photo with the camera or select one already on your phone. A temporary copy remains on your phone while you review it. If you discard it, that copy is deleted. If you tap “Save receipt,” the app keeps a JPEG in its private phone storage and sends a resized copy for receipt analysis. Re-analysis sends the saved receipt again and may include top and bottom detail crops.
  • Purchase details: Analysis returns store names, dates, items, prices and totals. These results, your edits, reports and in-app workbooks are stored on your phone, separated by signed-in Google account. You can export an Excel file to a location you choose.
  • Usage limits: To enforce daily analysis limits, our service stores a pseudonymous, cryptographically derived account identifier, the day, a usage count and an update time in Google Cloud Firestore. It also stores a global daily count. This is not a receipt or purchase database. Daily counts reset for limit calculations, but the records do not currently expire automatically.
  • Queued analysis: Version 1.1 and later temporarily store an uploaded image and its extracted result in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket in the EU. Google Cloud Firestore stores a random-looking job identifier, a pseudonymous account identifier, status, processing-attempt count, timestamps, image checksum and the quota reservation. Google Cloud Tasks stores the job identifier for dispatch and retry; its task payload does not include the receipt image or purchase details. This temporary storage lets analysis wait during busy periods and lets the app retrieve results after a connection interruption.

Where receipt analysis happens

After you save a receipt, its resized image is sent over HTTPS to our Google Cloud Run service in the EU. The service sends the image and an extraction request to DeepSeek, the current external AI provider, and returns extracted details to the app. A receipt may contain a store or address, purchase date, transaction identifiers, payment-method text and other personal information. Do not save a receipt containing information you do not want to submit for analysis.

External AI processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, including in China. Our service temporarily stores queued images, analysis results and job status only to complete the requested analysis; it does not maintain a long-term purchase-history database. Earlier app versions use a direct request that processes images and responses in memory without this queue storage. DeepSeek may handle submitted inputs under its applicable service terms and retention practices, which we do not control. Deleting a local receipt does not guarantee deletion of data already processed by DeepSeek. Read DeepSeek's published privacy information.

Purpose, sharing and security

From version 1.4, each account's private archive on the phone stores a fixed main currency, the original receipt currency and amounts, review flags, and any exchange rate used. Existing archives remain in EUR. For a foreign-currency receipt, the app requests a rate directly from Frankfurter over HTTPS, sending only source and destination currency codes and, when available, the purchase date. It does not send Frankfurter a photo, prices, store, Google account identifier or app-service credential. Frankfurter and its network/hosting providers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Purchase-date rates are preferred; a latest available rate may be used when a historical rate is unavailable. The selected rate and its date are stored locally so later rate changes do not alter saved totals. These are indicative reference rates, not a bank's actual conversion charges. If conversion is unavailable, original prices remain saved and that receipt is excluded from converted totals until a rate is obtained.

We use account information to provide sign-in and keep accounts separate, receipt images to extract purchase details, and pseudonymous usage records to enforce service limits. We do not run in-app advertising, advertising analytics or interaction-history tracking, and we do not sell receipt or account data.

Data leaves the phone only for the services described above or when you choose an external Excel export location. Google supplies identity services and cloud hosting; DeepSeek processes receipt images for extraction; Frankfurter supplies reference exchange rates. The app uses Android private storage, requests camera access only when needed, disables Android app backup, and uses HTTPS for the hosted service and exchange-rate requests. No internet service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Storage, retention and your choices

Saved receipt photos and extracted data stay in the app on your phone until you delete a receipt, delete that signed-in account's local data, or uninstall the app. Removing a receipt also removes its linked items. Deleting account data in the app removes that account's local receipts, items and in-app workbooks; data for other accounts on the same phone remains. Excel files you exported outside the app must be deleted separately. The app's service session may remain valid for up to seven days unless replaced or expired. From version 1.2, session expiry does not prevent an already signed-in account from browsing its local archive; reconnecting Google sign-in is needed for new analysis. Explicit sign-out still requires sign-in to reopen that account's archive.

For queued analysis, the uploaded cloud image is deleted when processing finishes or is cancelled. The cloud result is deleted when the app confirms that it saved the result locally. If the phone does not retrieve it, the job becomes inaccessible 24 hours after submission. Storage lifecycle rules delete remaining images and results, and Firestore expiry rules delete job metadata; these cleanup mechanisms are asynchronous and may take additional time after expiry. We do not enable object versioning or soft-delete retention for the temporary receipt bucket. Deleting account data also cancels that account's temporary jobs and deletes their cloud images and results. The revised app presents an updated agreement before it uses queued analysis.

From version 1.2, local account deletion does not wait for an internet connection. If cloud cleanup cannot finish, the phone privately keeps an account-scoped cleanup request and a service credential to retry when the app runs online; it does not keep the deleted receipts in that request. If the credential has expired, reconnect that account to complete cleanup. New analyses for that account wait until cloud cleanup is confirmed. The cleanup request is removed after confirmation; if you uninstall before reconnecting, the normal cloud expiry and retention rules above still apply.

The separate pseudonymous Firestore usage counters currently have no automatic expiry and are not removed by the app's in-app delete action, so deleting data does not reset the daily allowance. Contact us using the account and data deletion page to request review and removal of data we can identify and control. DeepSeek may retain submitted data under its own practices; we can help you route a provider-related deletion request but cannot promise that deleting local data removes provider-held copies.

You may decline the app's first-run agreement and exit without starting Google sign-in or a receipt upload. You can also view and delete individual receipts in the app. For privacy questions or applicable access, correction or deletion requests, contact deepmwizard@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request; please do not email receipt photos, passwords or payment details.

Children and changes

This app is intended for adults and is not designed for children. If the app's data handling changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes may also require an updated in-app disclosure or consent.
